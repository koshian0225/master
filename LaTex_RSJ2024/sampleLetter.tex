\begin{abstract}
    PMOD-Net uses monocular camera 3D maps for 3D obstacle detection, but struggles with dynamic obstacles such as people
and cars. In this study, we focus on the complementary relationship between monocular depth estimation (MDE) and 3D map.
MDE provides the relative depth of all objects in an image, and 3D map provides absolute distances of static objects. We propose
a novel method to enhance dynamic obstacle detection by integrating MDE and 3D maps. The proposed method reduces the
distance estimation error by 0.85 m compared to PMOD-Net.
\end{abstract}
